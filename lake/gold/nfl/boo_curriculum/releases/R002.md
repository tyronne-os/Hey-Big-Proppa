## How lessons are graded

- **Teach**: chance of profit in every tested season ≥ 60% (and ≥ 50% in every study), in profit in each season, hit rate above the price-implied probability, ≥ 1,000 slips.
- **Watch**: ≥ 30% chance of profit in every season, or a profit the 95% range supports.
- **Fragile**: profitable overall (95% range above zero, ≥ 1,000 slips) but it lost money in at least one tested season.
- **Avoid**: the whole 95% range is below zero, ≥ 1,000 slips.

> Every study replays the same real NFL seasons, so extra studies average out the betters' randomness, not what really happened. A lesson taught on two seasons can fail on a third: that is what releases are for. Lessons are priors for MY BOO, JIMMY and JEV, not guarantees.
